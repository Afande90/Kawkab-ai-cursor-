# M1.7 owner check

Date: 1 Nov 2026 (Asia/Dubai).

## What this milestone contains

- **Wake word (27):** say "Kawkab". A foreground service with a notification listens on the phone (sherpa-onnx keyword spotting behind Silero VAD). Off by default; turn it on in Settings → Phone access → Wake word. Nothing is recorded or sent. After a restart, open Kawkab once to start it again (Android allows a microphone service to start only while the app is in front).
- **Continuous conversation (28):** after the wake word, a tone, then Kawkab listens, replies by voice sentence by sentence, and listens 8 s more for a follow-up without the wake word. Silence ends the talk. Talking over Kawkab stops it.
- **Hands-free actions (29):** alarms, timers, opening apps and calendar events just happen, with "Say undo or tap Undo within 10 seconds". Calls and messages are read aloud ("Call Abdalla Mubadala?") and wait for your tap. Optional "Confirm by voice" (Phone access, behind a warning): a spoken yes counts only with the screen on and the phone unlocked; a yes on a locked phone never sends. Several matching contacts are read as a numbered list; answer "the second one" or a name.
- Native libraries are now compressed in the APK: it fell from 134 MB to about 88 MB.

## What to do on a phone

1. Install the newest `build-…` pre-release APK from the repository's Releases page. (Until the signing key from M1.6 is set up, uninstall the old app first.)
2. Make Kawkab the default assistant (Phone access → Default assistant). The wake word then opens the panel straight away; without it, a notification asks for one tap.
3. Phone access → Wake word → Turn on. Allow the microphone. A "Kawkab is listening for its name" notification appears.
4. Lock nothing yet. Say **"Kawkab, set an alarm for 6"**: a tone, then the panel, then the alarm is set and Kawkab says so. Say **"undo"** within 10 seconds: the alarm is removed.
5. Say **"Kawkab, call Abdalla Mubadala"**: Kawkab reads "Call Abdalla Mubadala?" and waits. Tap to call. If several contacts match, it reads them as a numbered list; say "the first one".
6. Optional: Phone access → Confirm by voice → read the warning → Turn on. Repeat step 5 and say "yes" with the phone unlocked: the call starts. Lock the phone, wake Kawkab, ask again and say "yes": nothing is sent; it waits for a tap.
7. After a reply, say a follow-up within 8 s without "Kawkab". Then stay silent: the conversation ends.
8. Leave the wake word on for a few hours without charging, then open Phone access: the Wake word card shows wakes per hour and the phone's battery drop per hour. Please send those two numbers and how often it missed "Kawkab".

This machine has no phone. These steps were not run here.
